The son who killed the monster.

28 July, 2024

Hi, I'm a final-year philosophy major at Azim Premji University.

Along with philosophy, I am working with Jayneil on his cool side project called Sneak Peek. It's just the two of us, so It's a bit tricky to point out what I exactly do, but I am into a bit of everything. It's fun.

I enjoy thinking deeply about ideas and designing experience flows, which naturally drew me toward design. Also, there’s nothing quite like seeing an idea take shape and come to life, iykyk.

Beyond that, I’m curious about business, films, filmmaking, both national and international politics, ethics, efficiency, and decision-making.

When I am doing nothing, you can catch me sitting next to any window watching people or animals going through life.

Thank you for stopping by my corner of the web. I will spill my thoughts, learnings, and explorations here. I’ve put a lot of heart into crafting it as a reflection of myself. I hope you find something here that resonates with you.

Finally, I can't talk about this site without mentioning my friend Vivek. He helped me launch it on my birthday. I still can't believe it. It's the best gift ever.
